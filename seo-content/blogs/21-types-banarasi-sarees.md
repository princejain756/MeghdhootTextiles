## Blog Content (1000 words)

**Types of Banarasi sarees** – essential knowledge for any serious saree retailer.

Banarasi is not just one type.
It's a family of weaves from Varanasi.

Here's your complete guide.

---

### 1. Katan Silk Banarasi

**The original.**

- Pure silk warp and weft
- Heaviest, most luxurious
- Bridal first choice
- Price: ₹3,000-20,000+ wholesale

---

### 2. Organza Banarasi

**Modern luxury.**

- Sheer, lightweight silk
- Contemporary appeal
- Growing popularity
- Price: ₹2,000-8,000 wholesale

---

### 3. Georgette Banarasi

**Flowing elegance.**

- Crepe texture with zari
- Lighter weight
- Younger customer appeal
- Price: ₹1,500-5,000 wholesale

---

### 4. Tanchoi Banarasi

**Single warp technique.**

- Satin finish
- Subtle patterns
- More affordable
- Price: ₹1,200-4,000 wholesale

---

### 5. Tissue Banarasi

**Metallic shimmer.**

- Gold/silver tissue threads
- Very festive
- Trending
- Price: ₹1,500-5,000 wholesale

---

### 6. Butidar Banarasi

**Small motifs.**

- Butis across body
- Classic pattern
- Very traditional
- Price: ₹2,000-6,000 wholesale

---

**For bridal focus:**
