### Pricing for Export

**Factor in:**
- Product cost
- Shipping (₹1,500-3,000/kg to USA)
- Customs duties (7-15% typically)
- Insurance
- Payment processing

**Pricing formula:**
Indian wholesale × 2.5 to 3.5 = USA retail price

**Example:**
₹1,500 silk saree = $60-80 USA retail

### Legal Requirements

**Export license:**
IEC code required (free, online)

**Customs documentation:**
- Invoice
- Packing list
- Shipper's letter

**Import duties (USA side):**
7-15% for most textiles
Customer usually pays

## FAQs

### 1. Do I need export license for sarees?
IEC code required. Free registration online.

### 2. What is shipping cost to USA?
₹1,500-4,000/kg depending on carrier.

### 3. What margins in USA market?
2.5-3.5x Indian wholesale price is typical.

### 4. What sarees sell best to NRIs?
Silk, Kanjivaram, Banarasi – premium traditional.

### 5. Can I sell on Amazon USA?
Yes, with proper registration and fulfillment setup.

### 6. How to find NRI customers?
Instagram, Facebook groups, NRI community events.
