**For the bride:**

**For wedding guests:**
- Soft silk

- Party georgette
- Price: ₹800-2,500 wholesale

**For family (mother, sister):**
- Quality silk

- Coordinated sets
- Price: ₹1,500-5,000 wholesale

### What Sells Best

**Colors for wedding season:**

1. Red (classic bridal)
2. Maroon/Wine
3. Pink (modern bridal)
4. Gold/Beige
5. Green (reception)
6. Pastel (Indo-western)

**Fabric preferences:**

1. Kanjivaram silk (40% of wedding sarees)
2. Banarasi (30%)
3. Soft silk (15%)
4. Georgette (10%)
5. Others (5%)

### Pricing Strategy

**Premium pricing applies:**

Wedding purchases are emotional.
Customers bargain less.
You can maintain better margins.

**Recommended markups:**

| Category | Wholesale | Retail | Markup |
|----------|-----------|--------|--------|
| Bridal silk | ₹5,000 | ₹10,000-12,000 | 100-140% |

### Display for Wedding Season

**Visual setup:**

- Dedicated wedding section
- Mannequins in bridal sarees
- Complete look styling
- Jewelry and accessories nearby
- Good lighting (crucial for silk)

**Appointment system:**
For bridal shopping, offer appointments.
Personal attention closes sales.

### Investment Planning

**For small boutique (₹1 lakh budget):**

- 15 bridal sarees (₹45,000)
- 20 reception/occasion (₹30,000)
- 25 guest sarees (₹25,000)
- Total: 60 wedding sarees

**For established store (₹3 lakh budget):**

- 40 bridal sarees
- 50 reception sarees
- 80 guest sarees
- Total: 170 wedding sarees

---

### Post-Season Strategy

**What happens to unsold stock:**

1. Discount sale (10-20% off)
2. Hold for next season (classic designs)
3. Summer wedding demand (smaller)
4. Export/NRI market

**Don't panic sell:**
Quality wedding sarees hold value.
Better to hold than deep discount.

## FAQs

### 1. When is wedding saree season?
October to February is peak. Order by September.

### 2. What colors sell most for weddings?
Red, maroon, pink for bridal. Wine, gold for reception.

### 3. How much to invest in wedding stock?
Increase regular inventory by 50-100% for wedding season.

### 4. What margin can I expect on wedding sarees?
100-150% markup is achievable during wedding season.

### 5. Kanjivaram or Banarasi – which sells more?
Kanjivaram in South India, Banarasi in North. Stock both.

### 6. How to handle unsold wedding sarees?
Light discount, hold for next season, or NRI market.
