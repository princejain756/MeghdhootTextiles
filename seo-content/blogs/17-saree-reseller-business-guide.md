## Blog Content (1100 words)

**Saree reseller business** is the lowest-investment entry into ethnic wear retail.

Here's how it works:
- You don't hold inventory
- You share catalogs
- Customer orders through you
- Supplier ships directly
- You keep the margin

Perfect for work-from-home.

---

### Reseller Platforms

**Popular options:**

| Platform | Investment | Margin |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| Meesho | ₹0 | 10-25% |
| GlowRoad | ₹0 | 10-25% |
| Shop101 | ₹0 | 10-25% |
| Direct suppliers | ₹10,000+ | 30-50% |

**How platforms work:**
1. Download app
2. Browse products
3. Share to customers (via WhatsApp, Facebook)
4. Customer orders → you get commission
5. Platform handles shipping, returns

---

### Pros and Cons

**Pros:**
- Zero inventory risk
- Work from anywhere
- No upfront investment
- Learn the market first
- Flexible timing

**Cons:**
- Lower margins (10-25%)
- No quality control
- Customer service challenges
- Platform dependency
- Competition is high

---

### Beyond Reseller Platforms

**Graduate to direct wholesale:**

As you grow, consider:
1. Buy inventory from [Meghdoot Textiles](/wholesale-sarees/)
2. Higher margins (40-80%)
3. Quality control
4. Build your brand
5. Scale properly

Start with reselling. Learn markets. Then invest.

---

### Tips for Success

1. **Niche down** – Specialize in segment
2. **Curate carefully** – Don't share everything
3. **Add value** – Styling tips, draping guides
4. **Quick response** – Answer queries fast
5. **Build WhatsApp groups** – Loyal customer base

## FAQs

### 1. How much can I earn as saree reseller?
Beginners: ₹5,000-15,000/month. Established: ₹25,000+.

### 2. Is Meesho good for saree reselling?
Yes, good starting point. Zero investment.

### 3. What investment for reselling?
₹0 on platforms. Or ₹10,000+ for light inventory.

### 4. How to find customers?
WhatsApp, Facebook groups, Instagram, friends/family.

### 5. Can I do this part-time?
Yes, flexible hours. Work from home.

### 6. When to buy inventory instead?
When you know what sells and want higher margins.
