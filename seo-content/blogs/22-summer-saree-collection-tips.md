### Best Summer Fabrics

| Fabric | Why | Price Range |
|--------|-----|-------------|
| Cotton | Breathable, classic | ₹350-800 |
| Linen | Super breathable | ₹800-1,500 |
| Chanderi | Sheer, light | ₹800-2,000 |
| Kota cotton | Checkered, airy | ₹600-1,200 |
| Mul cotton | Lightweight | ₹400-700 |

Avoid: Heavy silk, velvet, thick georgette.

---

### Summer Colors

**What sells:**
- White/off-white
- Pastels (peach, mint, lavender)
- Light blue
- Light yellow
- Soft pink

**Less demand:**
- Dark colors (black, navy)
- Very bright/hot colors

---

### Summer Stocking Strategy

**Shift inventory:**
- Reduce wedding/heavy stock
- Increase cotton and daily wear
- Stock office-appropriate options
- Light occasion wear for summer parties

**Quantity distribution:**
- 60% cotton and linen
- 25% light silk/chanderi
- 15% party/occasion

## FAQs

### 1. Which saree fabric is best for summer?
Cotton, linen, and Chanderi.

### 2. What colors sell in summer?
Whites, pastels, light colors.

### 3. Should I reduce silk stock in summer?
Yes, shift focus to lighter fabrics.

### 4. Is there demand for sarees in summer?
Yes, for daily wear, office, and light occasions.

### 5. How to store heavy sarees in summer off-season?
Cool, dry place with silica gel.

### 6. When to start summer collection?
Stock by March for April-June selling.
