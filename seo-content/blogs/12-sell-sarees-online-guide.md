### Instagram Selling Strategy

**Setting up:**

1. Business account (mandatory)
2. Clear bio (what you sell, contact method)
3. Consistent feed aesthetic
4. Story highlights (Categories, Prices, Reviews)
5. WhatsApp link in bio

**Content that works:**

- Saree flat lays with styling
- Model/mannequin photos
- Draping tutorials
- Behind-the-scenes
- Customer photos (UGC)
- New arrivals
- Festival collection reveals

**Posting frequency:**
- Feed: 1-2 posts daily
- Stories: 5-10 daily
- Reels: 3-5 weekly

### Photography Tips

**Basic requirements:**

- Good natural light (near window)
- Clean, uncluttered background
- Consistent style across all photos
- Show saree fully spread
- Close-up of pallu, border, blouse

**Budget setup:**

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Smartphone (good camera) | Already have |
| White/beige backdrop cloth | ₹300-500 |
| Mannequin (half body) | ₹1,500-3,000 |
| Ring light | ₹1,000-2,000 |
| **Total** | ₹3,000-5,500 |

**Photography style:**
Bright, clean, Instagram-aesthetic.
Show complete drape on mannequin.
Include detail shots.

### Pricing for Online

**Factor in:**

1. Product cost
2. Packaging (₹30-100/order)
3. Shipping (₹50-150/order)
4. Returns (factor 5-10%)
5. Marketing cost
6. Platform fees (if applicable)

**Pricing formula:**
(Product cost + Packaging + Average shipping) × 1.7 to 2.5 = Selling price

**Example:**
₹500 saree + ₹50 packaging + ₹100 shipping = ₹650
Selling price: ₹650 × 2 = ₹1,300

### Shipping and Logistics

**Courier options:**

| Courier | Best for |
|---------|----------|
| India Post | Budget, reach anywhere |
| Delhivery | Balanced cost/speed |
| Bluedart | Premium, fast |
| Shiprocket | Multi-courier aggregator |

**COD (Cash on Delivery):**
- 60-70% of Indian customers prefer COD
- Higher return rates (20-30%)
- Offer COD with conditions (order above ₹X)

**Packaging:**
- Protect against weather
- Brand packaging adds perceived value
- Include thank-you card
- Insert for social media follow

## FAQs

### 1. Can I sell sarees online without GST?
For small-scale Instagram selling, many start without. Required for marketplaces.

### 2. Which platform is best for selling sarees online?
Instagram for building brand, Amazon for volume, Meesho for zero investment.

### 3. How much investment for online saree business?
₹30,000-50,000 minimum for inventory, photography, and basic marketing.

### 4. Is COD necessary for online saree selling?
Highly recommended. 60-70% of Indian customers prefer COD.

### 5. How to take good photos of sarees?
Good lighting, clean background, full drape display, and close-up details.

### 6. What platform fees does Amazon charge?
Typically 15-20% commission depending on category.
