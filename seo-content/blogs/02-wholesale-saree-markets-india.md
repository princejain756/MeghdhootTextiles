### India's Major Saree Wholesale Hubs

India has several established wholesale textile markets:

1. **Surat, Gujarat** – The undisputed saree wholesale capital
2. **Delhi (Chandni Chowk)** – North India's wholesale hub
3. **Kolkata (Burrabazar)** – East India's trading center
4. **Mumbai (Crawford Market)** – Western India's fashion hub
5. **Bengaluru (Chickpet)** – South India's growing market
6. **Chennai (Sowcarpet)** – Tamil Nadu's wholesale district

Each has its strengths.

Let's break them down.

---

### Surat – The Textile Capital

**Why Surat dominates:**

Surat produces 90% of India's synthetic sarees.

Yes, ninety percent.

It's not just a market – it's a manufacturing hub.

**Key areas:**
- Sahara Darwaja
- Ring Road textile market
- Raghukul Market
- Surat Textile Market

**What you'll find:**
- Synthetic sarees (georgette, chiffon, net)
- Art silk sarees
- Printed and digital print sarees
- Budget to mid-range pricing

**Price advantage:**
Factory prices. Minimal markup.
Same designs you'll find marked up 30-50% elsewhere.

**Typical wholesale prices:**
- Georgette sarees: ₹400-800
- Art silk: ₹500-1,200
- Designer prints: ₹600-1,500

**Best for:**
- Volume buyers
- Synthetic fabric focus
- Budget and mid-range segments

**Challenges:**
- Travel and stay costs
- Need to visit personally
- Quality varies widely (inspect before buying)
- Returns can be difficult

---

### Delhi – Chandni Chowk Markets

**Why Delhi matters:**

Chandni Chowk has been a trading hub for centuries.

It's the gateway to North India's wholesale.

**Key lanes:**
- Kinari Bazaar (zari and embroidery work)
- Katra Neel (wholesale fabrics)
- Nai Sarak (general textiles)

**What you'll find:**
- Banarasi sarees and dupes
- Heavy work bridal sarees
- Net and embroidered sarees
- Lehengas and suits

**Typical wholesale prices:**
- Banarasi sarees: ₹1,200-5,000
- Heavy work: ₹1,500-4,000
- Net sarees: ₹600-1,500

**Best for:**
- Bridal and wedding inventory
- North Indian customer base
- Heavy work specialists

**Challenges:**
- Crowded, chaotic
- Heat and logistics
- Bargaining essential

---

### Kolkata – Burrabazar Hub

**Why Kolkata is significant:**

Eastern India's biggest wholesale market.

Strong in silk and cotton sarees.

**Key areas:**
- Burrabazar
- New Market
- Gariahat (retail prices)

**What you'll find:**
- Bengali silk sarees
- Tant and cotton varieties
- Jamdani (Bangladeshi borders)
- Affordable daily wear

**Typical wholesale prices:**
- Silk sarees: ₹800-3,000
- Cotton tant: ₹400-900
- Jamdani: ₹1,200-4,000

**Best for:**
- East India retailers
- Silk and cotton focus
- Bengali customer base

### Bengaluru – Chickpet Market

**Why Bengaluru is growing:**

South India's wholesale hub.
Strong in soft silk and cotton.

**Key areas:**
- Chickpet
- Avenue Road
- Commercial Street

**What you'll find:**
- Soft silk sarees
- Kanjivaram (from nearby Tamil Nadu)
- Cotton sarees
- Office wear

**Typical wholesale prices:**
- Soft silk: ₹800-2,500
- Cotton: ₹350-800

**Best for:**
- South India retailers
- Silk and cotton focus
- Quick access (if based in South)

### How to Buy from Wholesale Markets

**Before you go:**

1. Research what you need
2. Set a budget
3. Carry cash (many don't take cards)
4. Get GST documents ready
5. Bring a sample bag
6. Wear comfortable shoes (you'll walk!)

**At the market:**

1. Visit multiple shops
2. Don't buy at first shop
3. Bargain (10-20% off is normal)
4. Check quality carefully
5. Ask about return policy
6. Get GST invoice
7. Keep transport ready

**After buying:**

1. Pack properly
2. Track shipment if using transport
3. Inspect at delivery
4. Report defects immediately

## FAQs

### 1. Which is the best wholesale saree market in India?
Surat is the largest for synthetic sarees. Delhi for wedding wear. Kolkata for silk/cotton.

### 2. How much cheaper are wholesale markets vs retail?
Typically 30-60% cheaper than retail prices.

### 3. Do I need GST to buy from wholesale markets?
Not mandatory, but you won't get input tax credit without GST registration.

### 4. What is the minimum order in wholesale markets?
Varies by shop. Usually 10-50 pieces minimum.

### 5. Can I buy online instead of visiting markets?
Yes, many wholesalers like Meghdoot Textiles offer online ordering with pan-India delivery.

### 6. Is quality consistent in wholesale markets?
It varies. Always inspect before buying and buy from reputed shops.

### 7. Which market is best for Banarasi sarees?
Delhi (Chandni Chowk) and Varanasi for Banarasi specialties.

### 8. How to negotiate in wholesale markets?
Start at 20-30% below asked price. Settle at 10-15% discount typically.
