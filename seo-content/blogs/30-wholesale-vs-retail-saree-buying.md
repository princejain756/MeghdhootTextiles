### What's Included

**Wholesale:**
- Bulk pricing
- GST invoice for ITC
- MOQ requirement
- No trial/exchange usually

**Retail:**
- Single piece OK
- Trial allowed
- Exchange possible
- Higher price

---

### When Wholesale Makes Sense

- Buying to resell
- 30+ pieces needed
- Business registration
- Capacity to store
- Selling channel ready

---

### When Retail is OK

- Personal use
- 1-2 pieces
- Need to try before buying
- No storage space
- Testing before business

## FAQs

### 1. How much cheaper is wholesale?
50-150% depending on category.

### 2. Can I buy one piece wholesale?
No, MOQ applies (typically 30-50 pieces).

### 3. Is wholesale quality same as retail?
From good suppliers, yes. Quality is consistent.

### 4. Do I need GST for wholesale buying?
Not mandatory but recommended for ITC benefits.

### 5. What investment for wholesale start?
Minimum ₹25,000-50,000 for first order.

### 6. Where to buy wholesale?
Direct manufacturers like Meghdoot Textiles or wholesale markets.
