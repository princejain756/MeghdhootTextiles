### Why Stock Wedding Lehengas?

**The numbers speak:**

- Average wedding lehenga retail: ₹15,000-50,000
- Margin potential: 80-150%
- One lehenga profit = 10 saree profits
- Emotional purchase = less bargaining

**The challenge:**

- Higher investment
- Slower turnover
- Trend-sensitive
- Storage requirements

Worth it? Absolutely. If done right.

**Retail pricing formula:**

| Wholesale Cost | Retail Price | Strategy |
|---------------|--------------|----------|
| ₹5,000 | ₹12,000-15,000 | 140-200% markup |

| ₹20,000 | ₹40,000-55,000 | 100-175% markup |

**Key insight:**
Higher the wholesale cost, higher the absolute profit, even if percentage is similar.

### What to Look For

**Quality markers:**

1. Stitching quality (check seams)
2. Embroidery evenness
3. Stone/sequin security
4. Fabric weight and fall
5. Dupatta coordination
6. Choli finishing

**Complete sets include:**

- Lehenga skirt (semi-stitched or stitched)
- Choli/blouse (unstitched or semi-stitched)
- Dupatta (coordinated)

### Common Buying Mistakes

**1. Buying too late**
Stock by September. Don't scramble in November.

**2. Ignoring trends**
2024 trends: Pastels, organza, minimal embroidery. Stock accordingly.

**3. Only stocking red bridal**
Pink, wine, peach are equally popular now.

**4. Ignoring accessories**
Stock matching dupattas, jewelry option.

**5. Overbuying first time**
Start with minimum MOQ. Test what sells.
