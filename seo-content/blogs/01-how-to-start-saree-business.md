**How to start a saree business** is one of the most common questions I get from first-time entrepreneurs.

And I get it.

Sarees are India's most-worn ethnic garment.

Billions of rupees in annual sales.

If you're thinking about starting a saree business, you're thinking smart.

### Why Start a Saree Business?

Here's the reality of the saree market in India:

**Evergreen demand.**
Women buy sarees for every occasion.
Weddings, festivals, daily wear, office, parties.
There's no off-season for sarees.

**Multiple price points.**
You can sell ₹300 cotton sarees or ₹50,000 bridal sarees.
Or both.
There's a customer for every price point.

**Repeat customers.**
Women don't buy one saree.
They buy dozens over their lifetime.
One happy customer means years of sales.

**Low spoilage.**
Unlike food or fashion with strict seasons, sarees don't spoil.
Inventory management is simpler.

---

### Types of Saree Businesses

Before you start, decide your model:

**Physical Retail Store**
- Traditional showroom or shop
- High footfall locations
- Higher investment (₹5-20 lakhs typically)
- Face-to-face customer relationships

**Online Saree Business**
- E-commerce website or marketplace selling
- Lower overhead costs
- Pan-India reach
- Investment: ₹1-5 lakhs

**Instagram/WhatsApp Selling**
- Social commerce model
- Minimal investment (₹50k-2 lakhs)
- Personal brand building
- Flexible, work-from-home option

**Reseller/Dropshipping**
- No inventory holding
- Commission-based model
- Platforms like Meesho, GlowRoad
- Lowest investment (₹10k-50k)

**Wholesale Distribution**
- B2B model
- Supply to retailers
- Higher capital requirements
- Volume-based margins

### How Much Capital Do You Need?

Let's be realistic about investment:

**Minimum Viable Start (Online/Reseller)**
- Initial inventory: ₹50,000
- Website/marketing: ₹20,000
- GST + legal: ₹10,000
- **Total: ₹80,000 - 1 lakh**

**Small Boutique**
- Initial inventory: ₹2-3 lakhs
- Rent deposit: ₹50,000 - 1 lakh
- Interior/setup: ₹50,000
- GST + legal: ₹15,000
- **Total: ₹3-5 lakhs**

**Established Retail Store**
- Inventory: ₹5-10 lakhs
- Rent/deposit: ₹2-3 lakhs
- Interior: ₹1-2 lakhs
- Staff: ₹50,000/month
- **Total: ₹10-20 lakhs**

Start small.
Grow based on what sells.
Don't over-invest on day one.

### Understanding MOQ and Pricing

**What is MOQ?**

Minimum Order Quantity – the smallest quantity a wholesaler will sell.

Typical MOQ in saree wholesale:
- Cotton sarees: 50 pieces
- Silk sarees: 25-30 pieces
- Designer: 20-25 pieces

**Wholesale pricing structure:**

| Category | Wholesale Price | Retail Price | Your Margin |
|----------|----------------|--------------|-------------|
| Cotton | ₹350-500 | ₹600-800 | 40-60% |
| Silk | ₹1,000-2,500 | ₹2,000-4,500 | 50-80% |
| Designer | ₹1,500-3,000 | ₹3,000-5,500 | 60-100% |

**Volume discounts:**
Most wholesalers offer 5-15% additional discount at higher quantities.
Always negotiate.

### GST Registration and Compliance

**Do you need GST registration?**

If your turnover exceeds ₹40 lakhs (₹20 lakhs for services), yes.

But I recommend registering even if below threshold:
- Required for wholesale purchases
- Looks professional to customers
- Input tax credit benefits

**GST rates for sarees:**
- Cotton sarees: 5%
- Silk sarees: 5%
- Most sarees fall in 5% slab

**Documents needed for GST:**
- PAN card
- Aadhaar
- Business address proof
- Bank account details
- Photos

**Also consider:**
- Shop Act license (for physical store)
- FSSAI (not needed for textiles)
- Current account for business

### Profit Margins Explained

Let's do the math on a sample order:

**Example: Cotton saree business**

- Buy 100 cotton sarees at ₹400 each = ₹40,000
- Sell at ₹650 each × 80 sold = ₹52,000
- Gross profit: ₹12,000
- After expenses (rent, staff, marketing): ₹6,000-8,000 net

**Example: Silk saree segment**

- Buy 50 silk sarees at ₹1,500 each = ₹75,000
- Sell at ₹2,800 each × 40 sold = ₹1,12,000
- Gross profit: ₹37,000
- After expenses: ₹20,000-25,000 net

Higher investment = higher returns (with higher risk).

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Mistake 1: Starting too big**
Start small. Test the market. Scale gradually.

**Mistake 2: Ignoring cash flow**
Don't tie up all capital in inventory. Keep reserves.

**Mistake 3: Poor supplier selection**
Verify GST, quality, and reliability before bulk orders.

**Mistake 4: No niche focus**
Trying to sell everything to everyone fails. Find your niche.

**Mistake 5: Ignoring online presence**
Even physical stores need WhatsApp catalogs and social media.

**Mistake 6: Underpricing**
Don't compete on price alone. Compete on service and quality.

## FAQs

### 1. How much investment is needed to start a saree business?
Minimum ₹50,000-1 lakh for online/reseller model. ₹3-5 lakhs for small boutique. ₹10-20 lakhs for established retail store.

### 2. Is saree business profitable?
Yes, margins range from 40-100% depending on segment. Daily wear offers consistent volume, premium offers higher per-piece profit.

### 3. Do I need GST registration for saree business?
Required if turnover exceeds ₹40 lakhs. Recommended even below threshold for wholesale purchasing and professional image.

### 4. Where can I buy wholesale sarees?
Surat markets, direct manufacturers like [Meghdoot Textiles](/wholesale-sarees/), B2B platforms, and textile exhibitions.

### 5. What is MOQ in saree wholesale?
Minimum Order Quantity – typically 30-60 pieces depending on saree type and supplier.

### 6. Can I start saree business from home?
Yes, many successful sellers operate via WhatsApp, Instagram, and reseller platforms from home.

### 7. Which sarees sell the most?
Cotton sarees for daily wear (volume), silk for weddings (margins), and designer for boutiques (premium).

### 8. How to find customers for saree business?
Social media marketing, local WhatsApp groups, word-of-mouth, marketplace presence, and influencer collaborations.

### 9. What margins can I expect in saree retail?
40-60% on cotton, 50-80% on silk, 60-100% on designer sarees.

### 10. How long does it take to become profitable?
Typically 6-12 months for new businesses to stabilize and become consistently profitable.
