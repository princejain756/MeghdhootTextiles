## Blog Content (1100 words)

**Georgette vs chiffon saree** – two fabrics that confuse even experienced retailers.

Both are lightweight, flowy, and popular for party wear.

But they're not the same.

Here's the complete comparison.

---

### Fabric Basics

**Georgette:**
- Crinkled, textured surface
- Slightly heavier than chiffon
- More opaque
- Made from silk or polyester
- Better durability

**Chiffon:**
- Smooth, sheer finish
- Extremely lightweight
- More transparent
- Made from silk or synthetic
- Delicate

---

### Visual Differences

| Factor | Georgette | Chiffon |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Texture | Pebbled, crinkled | Smooth, flat |
| Transparency | Less sheer | More sheer |
| Weight | Slightly heavier | Very light |
| Drape | Structured flow | Floaty flow |
| Look | Elegant | Ethereal |

---

### Which Sells Better?

**Georgette wins for:**
- Everyday party wear
- Working women
- Sarees with heavy work
- Pan-India appeal
- Year-round demand

**Chiffon wins for:**
- Evening events
- Summer wear
- Printed sarees
- Younger customers
- Premium positioning

### Stocking Recommendations

**For general retail:**
- 70% georgette
- 30% chiffon

**For premium boutique:**
- 50% georgette
- 50% chiffon

**For online selling:**
- Focus on georgette (easier to photograph)
- Chiffon for premium listings

---

### Customer FAQ

**"Which is more comfortable?"**
Both are comfortable. Chiffon is lighter for hot weather.

**"Which drapes better?"**
Georgette for structured drape. Chiffon for floaty look.

**"Which is easier to maintain?"**
Georgette is slightly more durable.
