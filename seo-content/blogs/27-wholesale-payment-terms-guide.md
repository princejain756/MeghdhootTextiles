## Blog Content (800 words)

**Wholesale payment terms** vary widely.

Understanding them helps you plan cash flow and negotiate.

Here's the standard landscape.

---

### Common Payment Terms

**1. Full Advance (100%)**
- First-time orders
- Small buyers
- New relationships
- Safest for supplier

**2. Part Advance (50%)**
- Most common
- 50% to confirm, 50% before dispatch
- AT [Meghdoot Textiles](/wholesale-sarees/), we use this

**3. Post-Dated Cheque (PDC)**
- Credit against cheque
- 15-30 days typical
- For established buyers

**4. Open Credit**
- 30-60 days credit
- Long-term relationships only
- Based on track record

---

### What to Expect as First-Time Buyer

**Reality:**
Most suppliers want 100% or minimum 50% advance for first orders.

**After 3-5 orders:**
- Trust builds
- Better terms possible
- Negotiation opens

---

### Negotiation Tips

1. Pay on time consistently
2. Order regularly
3. Increase order size gradually
4. Ask for credit after building history
5. Provide business references

---

### Managing Cash Flow

**Tips:**
- Budget for advance payments
- Schedule orders around collection
- Build reserves
- Don't overstock

---

## FAQs

### 1. Do wholesalers take COD?
Most don't for first orders. Sometimes after relationship building.

### 2. What is standard advance payment?
50-100% for new buyers.

### 3. How to get credit terms?
Build relationship with timely payments over 3-6 orders.

### 4. Can I pay by UPI/NEFT?
Yes, most wholesalers accept digital payments.

### 5. What if I can't pay full advance?
Start small within your capacity.

### 6. Are payment terms negotiable?
After building trust. Rarely for first order.
