### Cotton Saree Care

**Washing:**
- Machine wash OK (gentle cycle)
- Use mild detergent
- Cold water
- Don't mix with heavy fabrics

**Drying:**
- Air dry in shade
- Avoid direct sunlight (fading)
- Dry horizontally if possible

**Ironing:**
- Cotton setting (medium-high heat)
- Iron while slightly damp
- Best results

**Storage:**
- Fold along pleats
- Store in cotton cloth
- Avoid plastic bags

---

### Silk Saree Care

**Washing:**
- Dry clean recommended
- Hand wash in cold water only
- Very gentle
- No scrubbing

**Drying:**
- Never wring
- Roll in towel to remove water
- Air dry in shade
- Never direct sun

**Ironing:**
- Low heat
- Iron on reverse side
- Through cotton cloth

**Storage:**
- Muslin cloth wrap
- Refold every 3-4 months
- Silica gel sachets

---

### Georgette/Chiffon Care

**Washing:**
- Hand wash only
- Cold water
- Mild liquid detergent

**Drying:**
- Never wring
- Hang to drip dry
- Shape while drying

**Ironing:**
- Very low heat
- Or steam
- Handle carefully

**Storage:**
- Hang if possible
- Fold gently if needed
- Avoid crushing

---

### General Tips

**Do:**
- Read care labels
- Separate colors first wash
- Use mild detergent
- Air dry when possible

**Don't:**
- Use bleach
- Use hot water on silk
- Expose to direct sun
- Store in plastic

## FAQs

### 1. Can silk sarees be washed at home?
Hand wash in cold water is possible. Dry clean recommended for heavy work.

### 2. How to remove stains from silk?
Cold water immediately. Professional cleaning for tough stains.

### 3. How often to refold stored sarees?
Every 3-4 months to prevent crease damage.

### 4. Can I iron georgette directly?
Use very low heat or steam through cotton cloth.

### 5. Best storage for long-term?
Muslin cloth wrap in cool, dry place. Silica gel for humidity.

### 6. How to prevent color fading?
Wash in cold water, dry in shade, avoid sun exposure.
