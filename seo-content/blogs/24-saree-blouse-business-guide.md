### Business Models

**1. Blouse piece selling:**
- Included with saree (already done)
- Separate blouse fabrics
- Designer blouse pieces

**2. Stitching service:**
- Partner with tailor
- Offer stitching with purchase
- Revenue share or fixed fee

**3. Ready-made blouses:**
- Pre-stitched blouses
- Various sizes
- Quick add-on sale

### Upselling Strategy

**With every saree sale:**
- "Want matching blouse stitched?"
- "We have ready-made options"
- "Designer blouse would elevate this"

Average add-on: ₹200-500 per saree sale.

### Stitching Service Setup

**Options:**
1. In-house tailor (higher control)
2. Partner tailor nearby (lower overhead)
3. Network of tailors (scalability)

**Revenue model:**
Keep 20-30% of stitching fee.
Or fixed monthly rent from tailor.

## FAQs

### 1. Is blouse business profitable?
Yes, high-margin add-on. 50-100% margins on ready-made.

### 2. What is MOQ for blouse pieces?
20-50 pieces typically.

### 3. Should I offer stitching service?
Yes, adds convenience and revenue.

### 4. What size range for ready-made?
32-44 blouse sizes cover most customers.

### 5. What blouse styles sell?
Basic, readymade matching the saree trend.

### 6. How to find blouse wholesale?
Same wholesalers often supply. Ask your saree supplier.
