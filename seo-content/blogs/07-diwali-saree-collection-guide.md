Here's your complete Diwali inventory guide.

### When to Start Stocking

**Timeline:**

| Month | Action |
|-------|--------|
| July | Review last year's sales data |
| August | Finalize budget, identify supplier |
| September | Place orders, first batch arrives |
| October | Display ready, marketing starts |
| Diwali week | Peak sales, reorder if needed |

Order at least 6-8 weeks before Diwali for best selection.

---

### What Colors Sell for Diwali

**Top Diwali colors:**

1. **Red** – Traditional, auspicious
2. **Gold/Golden** – Festive shimmer
3. **Pink** – Modern festive
4. **Purple/Wine** – Elegant evening
5. **Yellow** – Dhanteras favorite
6. **Orange** – Vibrant celebrations
7. **Green** – Prosperity symbolism

**Avoid:** Black (though it sells for parties, not traditional Diwali)

### Price Points to Cover

**Recommended price distribution:**

- Budget (under ₹1,500 retail): 30%
- Mid-range (₹1,500-3,500): 40%
- Premium (₹3,500-8,000): 25%
- Luxury (₹8,000+): 5%

Cover all segments. Different customers, different budgets.

---

### Quantity Planning

**For small boutique (₹1 lakh budget):**

- 30 silk sarees (₹1,500 avg wholesale)
- 25 cotton silk (₹600 avg)
- 20 designer (₹1,200 avg)
- Total: 75 sarees

**For established store (₹3 lakh budget):**

- 100 silk sarees
- 75 cotton silk
- 50 designer
- 25 premium
- Total: 250 sarees

---

### Gift Segment Strategy

Diwali = gifting season.

**Stock for gifting:**
- Pre-packed sarees with blouse piece
- Gift box options (extra ₹50-100)
- Corporate gifting sets
- Combo offers (saree + accessories)

Gifting segment can be 30-40% of Diwali sales.

---

### Marketing for Festival Season

**Before Diwali:**
- Social media countdown posts
- WhatsApp broadcast to existing customers
- Early bird discounts
- New arrival announcements

**During Diwali week:**
- Festival outfit posts
- Customer photo shares
- Last-minute availability updates
- Express dispatch offers

---

### Common Festival Stocking Mistakes

1. **Ordering too late** – Best choices gone by October
2. **All premium, no budget** – Miss volume sales
3. **Ignoring gift packaging** – Lost opportunity
4. **No marketing calendar** – Customer forgets you
5. **Overstocking single style** – Diversity matters

---

### Post-Diwali Strategy

Don't panic if unsold:

- Karva Chauth follows shortly after
- Wedding season begins November
- Discount sale attracts bargain hunters
- Hold premium for next pujas

Festival sarees are versatile. They sell beyond Diwali.

## FAQs

### 1. When should I order for Diwali?
6-8 weeks before Diwali. August-September is ideal.

### 2. What colors sell best for Diwali?
Red, gold, pink, yellow, and green are top sellers.

### 3. How much should I invest for Diwali stock?
Increase normal inventory by 50-100% for festival season.

### 4. Are silk sarees essential for Diwali?
Yes, silk is the preferred fabric for Diwali celebrations.

### 5. How to handle unsold festival stock?
Discount for Karva Chauth, wedding season, or end-of-year sale.

### 6. Should I stock gift packaging?
Yes, gifting is 30-40% of Diwali saree sales.
