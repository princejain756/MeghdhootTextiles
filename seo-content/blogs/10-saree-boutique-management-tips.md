### Inventory Management

**The 80/20 rule applies:**
80% of your sales come from 20% of your products.

**How to implement:**

1. Track what sells (use Point of Sale software)
2. Reorder fast-movers quickly
3. Discount slow-movers after 3 months
4. Don't emotionally attach to unsold pieces

**Optimal inventory turns:**
- Daily wear: 4-6x per year
- Occasion wear: 2-3x per year  
- Bridal: 1-2x per year

### Stock Organization

**Category arrangement:**

| Section | % of Space | Location |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| Daily wear | 30% | Accessible front |
| Occasion | 35% | Prime display |
| Bridal/Premium | 25% | Dedicated area |
| Clearance | 10% | Side section |

**Color organization:**
- Group by color within categories
- Easier browsing for customers
- Better visual appeal

---

### Visual Display

**Display rules:**

1. **Mannequins** – Dress 2-3 with featured sarees, change weekly
2. **Lighting** – Invest in good lighting, crucial for silk
3. **Height** – Display from eye-level to knee-level
4. **Rotation** – Move sarees around to keep fresh look
5. **Instagram corner** – Create photo-worthy spot

**Season updates:**
- Change displays for festivals
- Special wedding season setup
- Seasonal color themes

1. Fabric identification
2. Saree draping basics
3. Price range awareness
4. Customer handling

- Customer feedback scores

### Customer Service Excellence

**What makes customers return:**

1. **Knowledge** – Staff who can explain fabrics, occasions
2. **Patience** – Let customers browse without pressure
3. **Honesty** – "This color suits you" vs "Everything looks good"
4. **Memory** – Remember regular customers' preferences
5. **Follow-up** – WhatsApp updates on new arrivals

**What drives customers away:**

- Pushy selling
- Ignoring budget constraints
- Poor trial facility
- Unprofessional behavior

## FAQs

### 1. How often should I change saree displays?
Weekly for mannequins, monthly for sections.

### 2. What inventory software is best for saree shops?
Tally, Zoho Inventory, or specialized retail POS systems.

### 3. How much staff do I need?
1-2 for small boutique, 4-6 for established store.

### 4. What training do saree shop staff need?
Fabric knowledge, customer service, draping basics, pricing awareness.

### 5. How to handle slow-moving inventory?
Discount after 3 months, bundle with fast movers, clearance sales.

### 6. How important is social media for boutique?
Very important. Instagram especially drives younger customers.
