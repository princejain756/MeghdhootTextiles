### Do You Need GST Registration?

**Mandatory registration if:**

- Annual turnover exceeds ₹40 lakhs (goods)
- ₹20 lakhs for special category states
- Selling interstate (online or to other states)

**Recommended even if below threshold:**

- Required for wholesale purchases with ITC
- Looks professional
- Enables interstate sales

---

### GST Rates on Sarees

**Current rates (as of 2024):**

| Product | GST Rate |
|---------|----------|
| Cotton sarees | 5% |
| Silk sarees | 5% |
| Synthetic sarees | 5% |
| Embroidered/Designer | 5% |
| Saree fall/pico | 12% |
| Petticoats | 12% |
| Blouse pieces (over ₹1000) | 12% |

**Good news:**
Most sarees fall in 5% slab – one of the lowest in retail.

---

### Input Tax Credit (ITC)

This is where GST benefits you.

**How ITC works:**

1. You buy sarees at ₹1,00,000 + 5% GST = ₹1,05,000
2. You pay ₹5,000 GST to supplier
3. You sell at ₹1,50,000 + 5% GST = ₹1,57,500
4. You collect ₹7,500 GST from customers
5. You pay government: ₹7,500 - ₹5,000 = ₹2,500

**Without proper invoice?**
You'd pay full ₹7,500. That's why GST invoices matter.

---

### Documents Needed for GST Registration

- PAN card
- Aadhaar card
- Business address proof
- Bank account details
- Passport photos
- Constitution of business (if partnership/company)

**Registration time:** 3-7 working days online

---

### Invoice Requirements

Your invoices must include:

1. Your GST number
2. Buyer's GST (if registered)
3. Place of supply
4. HSN code
5. GST rate and amount
6. Invoice number (sequential)
7. Your signature/seal

---

### Filing Returns

**Return frequency:**

| Type | For |
|------|-----|
| GSTR-1 | Outward supplies (monthly/quarterly) |
| GSTR-3B | Summary return (monthly) |
| GSTR-9 | Annual return (yearly) |

**Quarterly filing option:**
If turnover under ₹5 crores, you can opt for quarterly filing.

---

### Buying Wholesale Without GST Issues

**Always ask supplier for:**

1. Proper GST invoice
2. Their GST number (verify on GST portal)
3. Invoice with correct HSN code
4. E-way bill (if delivery value > ₹50,000)

### E-Way Bill Requirements

**Required when:**
- Transporting goods worth > ₹50,000
- Interstate movement of any value (some states)

**Generate at:** https://ewaybillgst.gov.in

### Common GST Mistakes

1. **Not asking for GST invoice** – Lose ITC
2. **Buying from unregistered dealers** – No ITC
3. **Late return filing** – Penalties
4. **Not matching invoices** – ITC denied
5. **Wrong HSN codes** – Compliance issues

## FAQs

### 1. What is GST rate on sarees?
5% GST on all sarees – cotton, silk, synthetic.

### 2. Do I need GST for small saree shop?
Mandatory if turnover > ₹40 lakhs. Recommended even before.

### 3. Can I claim GST paid on wholesale purchases?
Yes, through Input Tax Credit, if you have proper invoices.

### 4. What is HSN code for sarees?
HSN 5208-5212 for cotton, 5007 for silk, 5407 for synthetic.

### 5. Do I need e-way bill for sarees?
Yes, if transporting goods worth > ₹50,000.

### 6. Can I do GST filing myself?
Yes, but consider a CA or GST practitioner for compliance.
