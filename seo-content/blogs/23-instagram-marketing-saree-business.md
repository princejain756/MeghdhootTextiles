### Account Setup

**Basics:**
- Business account (insights access)
- Clear bio (what you sell + contact)
- Profile picture (logo or saree)
- WhatsApp link in bio
- Highlights (Categories, Prices, Reviews, Contact)

---

### Content Types

**What works for saree businesses:**

| Content Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|--------------|-----------|---------|
| Product posts | Daily | Sales |
| Reels | 3-5/week | Reach |
| Stories | 5-10/day | Engagement |
| Customer photos | Weekly | Trust |
| Behind-scenes | Weekly | Connection |

---

### Content Ideas

**Product posts:**
- Saree flat lays
- Mannequin displays
- Styling suggestions

**Reels ideas:**
- Saree unfolding
- Draping tutorials
- Before/after looks
- New arrivals reveal
- Day colors for festivals

**Stories:**
- Daily new arrivals
- Polls and quizzes
- Customer testimonials
- Limited stock alerts
- Behind-the-scenes

---

### Hashtag Strategy

**Use mix of:**
- Big hashtags: #saree #indianfashion (reach)
- Medium: #sareelove #ethnicwear (discovery)
- Niche: #handloomsaree #sareecollector (qualified)
- Location: #bangaloresarees #mumbaisaree

**Template:**
15-20 hashtags per post.
Mix of reach and niche.

---

### Engagement Tips

1. Respond to every comment
2. Reply to DMs quickly
3. Engage with similar accounts
4. Use polls in stories
5. Ask questions in captions

---

### Converting Followers to Buyers

**The funnel:**
1. Attract with reels
2. Engage with stories
3. Educate with posts
4. Convert via DM/WhatsApp

**Call-to-action:**
- "DM for price"
- "Link in bio"
- "WhatsApp for catalog"

---

### Instagram Ads

**When to start:**
After organic foundation (1000+ followers).

**Best performing:**
- Carousel of products
- Video content
- Festival collections

**Budget:**
Start ₹5,000/month.
Scale what works.

---

### Consistency is Key

Post every day.
Stories multiple times.
Reels 3-5 times weekly.

Quality + consistency = growth.

**Instagram marketing for saree business** – your digital storefront.
