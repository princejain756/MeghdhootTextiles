### Size Range to Stock

**Standard sizes:**
- S: Bust 34-36"
- M: Bust 36-38"
- L: Bust 38-40"
- XL: Bust 40-42"

**Plus sizes:**
- XXL: Bust 42-44"
- XXXL (3XL): Bust 44-46"
- 4XL: Bust 46-48"
- 5XL: Bust 48-50"

**Minimum stock recommendation:**
XXL and XXXL are essential.
4XL if you're positioning as plus-size specialist.

---

### What Plus Size Customers Want

**Design preferences:**

1. Comfortable fabrics (cotton, rayon)
2. Flattering A-line cuts
3. Longer lengths (cover hips)
4. Three-quarter or full sleeves
5. Darker prints/colors
6. Quality stitching

**What they avoid:**
- Tight fits
- Very bright colors (some prefer)
- Cheap fabric that pills
- Short lengths

### Pricing Plus Size

**Common approaches:**

1. **Same price as regular** – Customer friendly, volume approach
2. **Slightly higher** (₹50-100 more) – Covers extra fabric cost
3. **Premium positioning** – Higher price, better service

**My recommendation:**
Keep pricing same. Build loyalty.
Or add ₹50-100 only for XXL+.

### Marketing Plus Size

**What works:**

1. "All sizes available" messaging
2. Plus size model photos (not just mannequin)
3. Featuring real customers (with permission)
4. Inclusive language
5. Size chart with actual measurements

**What damages:**
- "Limited XL available"
- Hiding plus sizes in back
- Condescending language
- Only mannequin photos
