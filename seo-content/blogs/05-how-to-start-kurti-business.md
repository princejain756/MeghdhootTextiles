### Why Kurti Business Makes Sense

**Market facts:**

- Kurtis are now more popular than sarees for daily wear
- Lower investment than saree business
- Higher turnover rates
- Younger customer demographic
- Growing plus-size market

**Business advantages:**

- Lower per-piece cost = lower risk
- Easier to ship (vs sarees)
- More frequent repeat purchases
- Simpler sizing (vs sarees)

### Types of Kurti Businesses

**1. Instagram/WhatsApp Selling**
- Lowest investment
- Work from home
- Direct to consumer
- Start with ₹30,000-50,000

**2. Online Marketplace (Amazon, Meesho)**
- Platform handles customers
- Built-in traffic
- Competition is high
- Start with ₹50,000-1 lakh

**3. Physical Boutique**
- Higher investment
- Local customer base
- Personal relationships
- Start with ₹2-5 lakhs

**4. Wholesale/B2B**
- Supply to retailers
- Higher volumes
- Longer payment cycles
- Start with ₹3-5 lakhs

### Inventory Management

**Category mix:**

- 50% cotton/rayon basics (volume)
- 30% printed/embroidered (margins)
- 20% designer/anarkali (premium)

**Size distribution:**

- S: 15%
- M: 25%
- L: 30%
- XL: 20%
- XXL: 10%

*(Adjust based on your customer base)*

**Seasonal planning:**

- Festival kurtis: Stock 1 month before
- Summer: Lighter fabrics, sleeveless
- Winter: Full sleeves, darker colors
