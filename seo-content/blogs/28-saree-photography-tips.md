## Blog Content (900 words)

**Saree photography tips** for online sellers.

Good photos = Good sales.
Bad photos = No clicks.

Here's how to photograph sarees that sell.

---

### Basic Setup

**Equipment:**
- Smartphone (decent camera)
- Natural light or ring light
- White/neutral backdrop
- Mannequin or flat lay surface

**Budget:** ₹3,000-5,000 for complete setup.

---

### Lighting

**Natural light:**
- Near window, morning or late afternoon
- Diffused (avoid direct sunlight)
- Consistent direction

**Artificial:**
- Ring light (₹1,000-2,000)
- Two softbox lights (₹3,000+)
- No mixed lighting

---

### Ways to Display

**1. Flat lay:**
- Saree spread out
- Show pallu, border, blouse piece
- Easy, consistent

**2. Mannequin:**
- Half-body mannequin
- Shows drape
- More aspirational

**3. Model:**
- Best conversion
- Expensive
- For premium pieces

---

### Key Shots

For each saree:
1. Full saree (flat or draped)
2. Pallu close-up
3. Border detail
4. Blouse piece
5. Fabric texture

---

### Editing

**Keep it natural:**
- Color accuracy important
- Don't over-filter
- Consistent style
- Crop properly

**Apps:**
- Lightroom Mobile (free)
- Snapseed
- VSCO

---

### Instagram Optimization

**Format:**
- Square (1:1) for feed
- Vertical (4:5) for maximum size
- Stories (9:16)

**Consistency:**
- Same background
- Same lighting
- Brand aesthetic

---

### Common Mistakes

1. Dark/shadows on saree
2. Wrinkled fabric
3. Cluttered background
4. Over-editing colors
5. Low resolution

## FAQs

### 1. What camera for saree photography?
Good smartphone is enough. DSLR for premium quality.

### 2. What background color?
White or light neutral. Consistent.

### 3. Natural or artificial light?
Natural is best. Ring light as backup.

### 4. Should I iron sarees before photos?
Yes. Steam or dry iron. No wrinkles.

### 5. How many photos per saree?
Minimum 4-5. Full, pallu, border, blouse, detail.

### 6. Apps for editing?
Lightroom Mobile, Snapseed, VSCO.
