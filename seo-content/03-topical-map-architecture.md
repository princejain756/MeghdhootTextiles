## INTERNAL LINKING RULES

### Breadcrumb Pattern
```
Home > Wholesale Sarees > Cotton Sarees
Home > Wholesale Kurtis > Anarkali Kurtis
Home > Wholesale Lehengas > Bridal Lehenga
Home > Blog > How to Start Saree Business
```

### Contextual Linking Rules

1. **Pillar → Category (Always)**
   - Every pillar page links to ALL its category children
   - Use descriptive anchor text: "Browse our cotton sarees wholesale collection"

2. **Category → Pillar (Always)**
   - Every category links back to parent pillar
   - Anchor: "View all wholesale sarees"

3. **Category → Related Categories (2-3 links)**
   - Fabric categories link to occasion categories
   - Example: "Cotton sarees perfect for daily wear sarees"

4. **City Pages → Pillars + Categories**
   - Each city page links to all 3 pillars
   - Links to 2-3 most relevant categories

5. **Blog → Pillars + Categories (3-5 links)**
   - Every blog links to at least 1 pillar
   - Links to 2+ relevant category pages
   - Links to 1-2 city pages

6. **Brand Defense → All Pillars**
   - Every brand page links to all 3 pillars
   - Links to 2-3 high-value categories

### Anchor Text Patterns (Safe for India SERPs)

**Pillar Anchors:**
- "wholesale sarees collection"
