## FAQs – Wholesale Sarees

### 1. What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for wholesale sarees?
Our MOQ varies by fabric type. Cotton and georgette sarees have a 50-piece MOQ. Silk and designer sarees have 25-30 piece MOQ. Premium banarasi sarees can be ordered from 20 pieces. We can discuss flexible options for first-time buyers.

### 2. Do you provide GST invoice for wholesale saree orders?
Yes, absolutely. We are a GST-registered wholesaler and provide proper GST invoices with every order. This is essential for retailers who need compliant billing for their business records and input tax credit claims.

### 3. What is the dispatch time for wholesale saree orders?
Standard dispatch time is 2-5 working days from order confirmation and payment. During peak seasons (Diwali, wedding season), dispatch may take 1-2 extra days. We always communicate realistic timelines upfront.

### 4. Can I get wholesale sarees delivered anywhere in India?
Yes, we ship to all major cities and towns across India. Our logistics partners cover metros like Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad as well as Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. Shipping costs vary by location.

### 5. What payment methods do you accept for wholesale orders?
We accept bank transfers (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS), UPI payments, and cheque payments for established accounts. 50% advance is required to confirm orders, with balance payable before dispatch.

### 6. Can I get a catalog of your wholesale sarees?
Yes, we provide digital catalogs on request via WhatsApp. You can also visit our showroom in Bengaluru to view physical samples before placing bulk orders.

### 7. What is the profit margin on wholesale sarees for retailers?
Depending on the saree category and your local market, retailers typically earn 30-60% margins on our wholesale sarees. Cotton sarees offer volume-based margins while designer sarees offer higher per-piece margins.

### 8. Do you offer exclusive designs for retailers?
Yes, for orders above 500 pieces, we can work on exclusive color variations and design customizations. This helps retailers differentiate their inventory from competitors.

### 9. What is your return policy for wholesale saree orders?
Defective pieces (manufacturing defects) are replaced at no extra cost. We request photo/video evidence within 48 hours of delivery. No returns for buyer's remorse or styling preferences in wholesale.

### 10. How can I verify your wholesale saree quality before ordering?
We recommend first-time buyers order a smaller quantity (at MOQ) to check quality before scaling up. You can also visit our Bengaluru facility for physical inspection of samples.

### 11. Do you supply to online sellers on Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho?
Yes, many of our buyers are e-commerce sellers. We provide product images and descriptions that you can use for your listings. GST invoices help with marketplace compliance requirements.

### 12. What fabrics are trending in wholesale sarees currently?
Organza sarees are extremely popular for weddings and parties. Soft silk remains strong in South India. Cotton sarees have steady year-round demand. Linen sarees are growing, especially in summer months.
