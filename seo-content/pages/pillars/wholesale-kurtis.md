## FAQs – Wholesale Kurtis

### 1. What is the MOQ for wholesale kurtis?
Standard MOQ is 50 pieces for cotton and rayon kurtis. Designer and silk kurtis have 30-40 piece MOQ. We can discuss flexibility for first-time buyers testing our quality.

### 2. What sizes do you offer in wholesale kurtis?
We offer S, M, L, XL, XXL, and XXXL sizes. Not all designs are available in all sizes. Our bestsellers are usually stocked in the full size range. Plus-size kurtis (XXL-XXXL) are available in select designs.

### 3. Do you provide size labels on kurtis?
Yes, all our kurtis come with size labels attached. This makes inventory management easier for retailers and helps customers identify their size quickly.

### 4. What is the dispatch time for wholesale kurti orders?
Standard dispatch is 2-5 working days from order confirmation. During peak festive seasons, it may extend by 1-2 days. We communicate realistic timelines with every order.

### 5. Can I order mixed sizes in wholesale?
Yes, you can order mixed sizes within the same design. For example, 10 pcs in M, 15 in L, 15 in XL, and 10 in XXL for a 50-piece order of one design.

### 6. What is the profit margin for retailers on kurtis?
Retailers typically earn 40-70% margins on our wholesale kurtis. Cotton daily wear kurtis have volume-based margins while designer kurtis offer higher per-piece profits.

### 7. Do you offer exclusive kurti designs for retailers?
For orders of 300+ pieces, we can work on exclusive color combinations or minor design modifications to help you differentiate from competition.

### 8. Are your kurtis good for online selling?
Yes, many of our buyers sell on Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, and GlowRoad. We can provide high-quality product images for your listings on request.

### 9. What fabrics are trending in wholesale kurtis?
Rayon continues to dominate due to its soft feel and vibrant print absorption. Cotton remains strong for daily wear. Chanderi and Lucknowi work are popular for festive occasions.

### 10. Do you provide GST invoice for kurti orders?
Yes, we are GST-registered and provide proper tax invoices with every order. This is essential for retailers claiming input tax credits.

### 11. Can I visit your facility to check kurti quality?
Yes, you're welcome to visit our Bengaluru facility. Please schedule an appointment via WhatsApp so we can prepare samples for your review.

### 12. What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS), UPI, and cheques for established accounts. 50% advance confirms the order with balance due before dispatch.
