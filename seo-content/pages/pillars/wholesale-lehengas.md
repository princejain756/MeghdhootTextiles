## FAQs – Wholesale Lehengas

### 1. What is the MOQ for wholesale lehengas?
MOQ varies by lehenga category. Simple party wear lehengas: 20 pieces. Wedding lehengas: 15 pieces. Premium bridal lehengas: 8-10 pieces. Designer heavy work: 5 pieces.

### 2. Are lehengas sold as complete sets?
Yes, all our wholesale lehengas are complete sets including lehenga skirt, choli/blouse, and dupatta. You don't need to source these separately.

### 3. What sizes are available for wholesale lehengas?
Most lehengas are semi-stitched with free-size skirts that fit 28-40 waist. Cholis are unstitched or semi-stitched for customization. Some ready-to-wear options available in S/M/L/XL.

### 4. What is the dispatch time for lehenga orders?
Standard dispatch is 2-5 working days. During peak wedding season (October-January), dispatch may take 3-7 days due to high demand. Order early for guaranteed availability.

### 5. What is the profit margin on wholesale lehengas?
Lehengas offer 80-150% margins for retailers. A ₹3,000 wholesale lehenga typically sells for ₹6,000-8,000 retail. Bridal lehengas can command even higher markups.

### 6. Do you offer bridal lehengas in different colors?
Yes, bridal lehengas are available in traditional red/maroon plus trending colors like pink, peach, gold, wine, and pastels. Color availability varies by design.

### 7. Can I order sample lehengas before bulk order?
Yes, first-time buyers can order at MOQ quantity to assess quality before larger orders. Visit our Bengaluru showroom for direct sampling.

### 8. What fabrics are popular for wholesale lehengas?
Silk remains the bridal favorite. Velvet is strong for winter weddings. Georgette and net are popular for party wear due to lightweight comfort.

### 9. Do you provide GST invoice for lehenga orders?
Yes, we are GST-registered and provide proper invoices with every order. Essential for retailers claiming input tax credits.

### 10. When should I order for wedding season?
Order by August-September for October-December weddings. Order by November-December for January-February weddings. Early ordering ensures best selection.

### 11. Are your lehengas suitable for NRI customers?
Yes, many of our designs cater to NRI preferences. We can arrange international shipping for export buyers. Contact us for bulk export pricing.

### 12. Can you customize lehenga designs for bulk orders?
For orders of 50+ pieces of one design, we can customize colors, embroidery patterns, and fabric options. Allow 3-4 weeks for customized orders.
