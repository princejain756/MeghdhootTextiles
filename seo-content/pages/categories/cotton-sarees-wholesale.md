## FAQs – Cotton Sarees Wholesale

### 1. What is the MOQ for cotton sarees wholesale?
Standard MOQ is 50 pieces for most cotton saree varieties. Handloom cotton sarees have 30-piece MOQ due to higher value.

### 2. Are running blouse pieces included?
Yes, cotton sarees come with matching or contrast running blouse pieces as per design. Blouse piece length is typically 0.8-1 meter.

### 3. Do you have handloom cotton sarees?
Yes, we stock Mangalagiri, Chettinad, Khadi, and other handloom varieties. These command premium pricing and lower MOQ.

### 4. What prints are available in cotton sarees?
We have floral prints, geometric patterns, traditional motifs, abstract designs, and digital prints. Catalog available on WhatsApp.

### 5. Are cotton sarees good for online selling?
Excellent choice! Cotton sarees photograph well, ship easily, and have broad appeal. Perfect for e-commerce platforms.

### 6. What is dispatch time for cotton sarees?
Standard 2-5 working days from order confirmation. Cotton sarees are usually ready stock items.

### 7. Can I mix designs in a cotton saree order?
Yes, you can order different designs within the same cotton category as long as total quantity meets MOQ.

### 8. What is the profit margin on cotton sarees?
Retailers typically earn 40-60% margins. A ₹400 wholesale cotton saree sells for ₹650-800 retail.
