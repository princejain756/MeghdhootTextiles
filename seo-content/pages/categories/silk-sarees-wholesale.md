### 1. What is the MOQ for silk sarees wholesale?
MOQ varies by type. Art silk: 40 pieces. Soft silk: 30 pieces. Banarasi/Kanjivaram: 15-20 pieces due to higher value.

### 2. What's the difference between pure silk and art silk?
Pure silk is natural fiber with premium pricing. Art silk is synthetic with silk-like appearance at lower cost. Both have market demand.

### 3. Do silk sarees include blouse pieces?
Yes, all silk sarees include matching or contrast blouse pieces. Some premium sarees include unstitched blouse material.
